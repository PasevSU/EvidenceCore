# Commercial licensing / Платен лиценз

**Product:** PasevSU EvidenceCore

**Model:** Proprietary commercial license; public documentation repository; project-authored source is proposed for public visibility under proprietary commercial terms, but has not yet been uploaded.

**Not configured:** Price tiers, Stripe/PayPal checkout, tax/VAT invoices, key issuing, seat/device binding, renewals, trials, activation APIs and a customer portal. No claim is made that payment currently unlocks the software.

## Suggested licensing workflow

1. Customer selects an offered edition and receives complete pricing, duration, permitted device/user count, support, updates and refund terms.
2. Customer accepts the final commercial agreement and completes checkout through an authorized provider.
3. Vendor issues a receipt/invoice and makes a controlled installer or archive available.
4. If technical activation is added, validate signed licenses locally and offer an offline flow for sensitive evidence-processing environments.
5. Maintain revocation, renewal and customer-support processes without collecting evidence contents by default.
